Comprehensive Programs

What We Offer At Brighter Glory

From early creche care to primary graduation, we offer holistic services designed to nurture your child's complete growth.

Early Childhood & Creche

Ages 3 Months – 2 Years

Hygienic and loving infant care environment staffed by qualified early childhood caregivers.

  • Individualized sleeping cribs & quiet nap areas
  • Early sensory play & fine motor skills stimulus
  • Daily health & diaper tracking report for parents

Nursery Education (1 & 2)

Ages 3 – 5 Years

Foundational Nursery learning using wall mural phonics, interactive toys, rhymes, and numbers.

  • Jolly Phonics reading & pronunciation foundation
  • Mathematical logic & puzzle building (1-25+)
  • Rhymes, creative art, and social interaction

Primary School (Primary 1 - 6)

Ages 6 – 11 Years

Rigorous primary education preparing pupils for top secondary schools and national common entrance exams.

  • Mathematics, Science, English & ICT Literacy
  • Cultural studies, Civics & Character Building
  • Intensive Common Entrance & Olympiad Prep

Safe School Transportation

Akure Environs Coverage

Safe school bus service serving Oke Aro, Ifelere, Alagbaka, Ijapo, Oba-Ile, and central Akure zones.

  • Professional vetted drivers & onboard bus assistants
  • Reliable pickup and dropoff schedule
  • Doorstep or central junction pickup convenience
